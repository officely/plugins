# People and groups

Read the [MCP workflow](../officely/references/mcp-workflow.md) before using live tools, unless it is already in context.

## Retrieve the relevant behavior

- [Understanding access](https://docs.officely.app/using/people-and-access.md): choosing between roles, groups, and booking rules.
- [Manage people](https://docs.officely.app/using/manage-people.md): person lifecycle.
- [Managing access for a team](https://docs.officely.app/using/manage-groups.md): memberships and access through groups.
- [Giving people responsibilities](https://docs.officely.app/using/roles.md): roles, assignment scope, and timing.

## Approach the task

Identify the intended outcome: a person record, team membership, management responsibility, or booking arrangement. Retrieve the relevant access guidance before choosing the mechanism. Route booking arrangements to [Booking rules](../officely-booking-rules/SKILL.md).

Resolve people and groups against current account data. Inspect existing memberships and assignments, including their dates and scope, before creating replacements. For offboarding or a reported access loss, examine other assignments and groups that may still supply access rather than inferring the result from a single membership.

For changes spanning several people, keep the requested set explicit and report individual failures. Verify the resulting person, membership, or assignment state against the intended outcome; distinguish the stored change from effective access that still depends on other configuration.
