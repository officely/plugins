# Workplace inventory

Read the [MCP workflow](../officely/references/mcp-workflow.md) before using live tools, unless it is already in context.

## Retrieve the relevant behavior

- [Organise your workplaces](https://docs.officely.app/using/workplaces.md): hierarchy, resource facts, and supported changes.
- [Group desks and rooms](https://docs.officely.app/using/resource-groups.md): resource selection and grouping.
- [Close or retire a workplace](https://docs.officely.app/using/closures.md): closures and retirement effects.

## Approach the task

Resolve the existing hierarchy before proposing additions. Use the workplace guide to choose the entity for each part of the user's layout. Establish how the user expects each resource to be booked, then consult the guide's capacity and occupancy distinctions when choosing resource facts.

Before a move or structural change, retrieve the applicable restrictions and inspect the current parents and resource groups. Resolve duplicate names within the intended place or organisation. Change only the requested portion of the hierarchy.

For closures or retirement, inspect affected resources and bookings and retrieve the lifecycle guidance before choosing the action. After a change, verify parentage, resource facts, and relevant group membership. Use [Booking rules](../officely-booking-rules/SKILL.md) when the requested outcome concerns who may book, rather than the inventory itself.
