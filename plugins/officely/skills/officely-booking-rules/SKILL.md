# Booking rules

Read the [MCP workflow](../officely/references/mcp-workflow.md) before using live tools, unless it is already in context.

## Retrieve the relevant behavior

- [Control who can book a resource](https://docs.officely.app/using/booking-rules.md): rule selection, ordering, and overlapping settings.
- [Set up booking approvals](https://docs.officely.app/using/set-up-approvals.md): approver eligibility and request lifecycle.
- [Set booking limits](https://docs.officely.app/using/booking-limits.md): counting units and periods.

## Approach the task

Resolve the organisation, target resources, and audience. Read the existing rules in their current order before choosing whether to change a rule or add one. For approvals, also resolve the approver group and inspect its current membership; use [People and groups](../officely-people-and-groups/SKILL.md) when membership work is needed.

Translate the desired outcome into the settings or limits to change. Consult the relevant behavior above for how matching rules combine. Consider both the intended audience and anyone else covered by overlapping rules; do not assume a narrower rule automatically wins.

Discover the current create, update, or reorder contract for the chosen change. After execution, read the rule and its position back and assess the effective policy against the requested scenario. Report what was configured separately from any booking behavior that has actually been exercised.
